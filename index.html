Process

How voting works

Four quiet steps. One secret ballot. Your choice stays yours.

  1. 1

    Sign in

    Enter your registration number and email on the casting portal to open the booth. Registration numbers are case-sensitive.

  2. 2

    Verify with OTP

    Confirm it is you with a one-time code sent to your email — no shared passwords.

  3. 3

    Cast in private

    Mark your ballot for each position. Your selections are secret until you submit.

  4. 4

    Keep your receipt

    Receive a one-time receipt code so you can confirm your vote was recorded.

Tip: If your one-time code does not arrive, check your email spam or junk folder first. Request a new OTP only if it is still missing.
