# KASDA Vote

The public site for **kasdavote.com** — home of **Kapchorwa Students' Development Association** elections (*Ciiner Korenyo*).

It introduces KASDA Vote, explains how voting works, and shares status for **KASDA Elections 2026**. Polling has been postponed pending notice from the Electoral Commission through its official channels; offices on the ballot are not listed on this site. Only qualified voters cast ballots when the booth opens; casting happens on **[cast.kasdavote.com](https://cast.kasdavote.com)**, linked throughout the site.

The KASDA logo is included in `assets/` as `kasda-logo.jpg` and `favicon.jpg`, and appears in the site header and hero.
